Arizona ESA Program

AZ families: tuition fully covered with ESA

The state issues an Education Savings Account on your behalf. That money goes straight to Novatio. You never write a check to the school.

You never pay Novatio directly.

The Arizona ESA is a state-funded scholarship account, not a reimbursement. You do not pay tuition first and wait to get money back. The state deposits funds directly, and Novatio is paid from there. Your family pays $0 at every step.

1
You apply for the ESA through the Arizona Department of Education. We walk you through every step.
2
ADE approves your account and deposits your ESA funds. This covers Novatio's full tuition.
3
Novatio is paid directly from your ESA. Any remaining balance is yours for other approved educational expenses.

Common Questions

Everything you want to know.

Honest answers to the questions Arizona parents ask most.

Will the day be too structured for our family?⌄
Two hours of academics each morning, live with a Novatio teacher, then afternoon clubs run until 2 PM. After that, the day is yours. No homework, ever. Most homeschool-to-Novatio families keep their existing afternoon routines fully intact. Travel and appointments work fine.
How much of my day will I get back?⌄
Most homeschool parents tell us mornings open up by about three to four hours. Live academics run roughly 8 AM to 10 AM. After that, afternoon clubs run until 2 PM, and your child is engaged with their teacher and classmates the whole time.
Can my child still do co-ops, music lessons, and sports?⌄
Yes. The school day ends at 2 PM and there's no homework, ever. The platform travels with you, so appointments and travel days work fine.
How does Arizona's ESA work, and does my family qualify?⌄
Every Arizona K–12 student qualifies. No income test, no disability requirement, no minimum academic standard. The Arizona ESA is a state-funded scholarship account the Arizona Department of Education deposits on your behalf. ESA funds pay Novatio's $7,500 tuition directly, and any balance beyond tuition stays in your ClassWallet account for other approved expenses like tutoring, curriculum, or therapy. You never pay anything to Novatio.
How many hours a day does school take?⌄
Core academics take about 2 hours each morning. The full school day runs from 8 AM to 2 PM, with the remaining time for games, breaks, lunch, and afternoon clubs. There is no homework. Ever.
Is Novatio accredited?⌄
Yes. Novatio is accredited by Cognia, the leading accrediting organization for K–12 schools in the US and internationally. Cognia accreditation is recognized by colleges, universities, and employers worldwide.
Can my student work at their own pace?⌄
Yes, that's the entire point. Our AI platform adapts to each student's exact level in every subject simultaneously. A student can be at grade 3 in math and grade 7 in social studies, and the system adjusts automatically every day. No more being held back, no more getting left behind.
What if my child has an IEP or 504 plan?⌄

Novatio is built for students with IEPs, 504 plans, and a wide range of learning profiles, including students with dyslexia and those who've needed more support than a traditional classroom could offer.

Every Novatio student gets an individualized pace in every subject, extended time, frequent breaks, text-to-speech, and live teachers on-screen throughout the day.

For students who need more, our Special Education Support Program adds IEP and 504 coordination with your local school district, reading intervention, executive-functioning coaching, and extra 1:1 check-ins. Students who need intensive one-on-one dyslexia support can enroll in our One-on-One Dyslexia Intervention Program, taught by master's-level reading and dyslexia specialists. Clinical services like speech therapy, occupational therapy, and counseling are handled by outside providers.

These support services carry additional costs. Speak to our admissions team for details, and we're happy to talk through your child's specific situation before you apply.

How is Novatio different from other online schools in Arizona?⌄
Most online schools move the traditional model to a screen: same 6-hour day, same passive lectures, same homework. Novatio was built differently from the ground up. AI compresses academics into 2 focused hours, and the rest of the day is spent building real-world skills most schools never touch.
Do students actually socialize and make friends?⌄
Yes, Novatio is a real community. Students are on video together all day: morning games, lunch, and collaborative clubs. Many students say the friendships at Novatio are stronger than what they had at their previous school.
